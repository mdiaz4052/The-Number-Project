## Preparatory cautions, not comparative findings

The [GFT-1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.05881v2) specifies isotropy, a massless scalar, subdominant interactions and a parameter-qualified semiclassical recovery of Friedmann equations. That scope must not be silently expanded to general nonlinear GR.

The [CST-1 source record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725v4) explicitly reports corrections to equation (3) and the definition of n(x,y), noting that the published version still contains a typo. The corrected arXiv version controls this pilot. Its operator approximation on continuum-approximating causal sets is not, by itself, a derivation that full quantum dynamics selects such sets. The [CST-2 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1622v1) also flags stability concerns for the original four-dimensional operator. Do not generalize or discard that qualification without inspecting the calculation.

The [SIG-1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9607104v1) concerns a low-energy induced action, heavy constituents and black-hole entropy. It identifies a concrete construction to inspect, not a unique prediction of every Sakharov-style model. The metric/background assumptions, matter spectrum and regulator conditions must remain explicit in extraction.

The [REF-J](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004v2) and [REF-E](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.3716v2) abstracts describe conditional derivations; REF-E is explicitly linearized about pure AdS. Neither reference automatically certifies a missing arrow in one of the three comparison units.
