## 5. Common map and eligibility gates

For each unit construct P -> D -> C -> G -> Delta:

- P: primitives, including any geometry or causal structure already assumed.
- D: dynamics, action, operator, measure, state and boundary/initial conditions.
- C: the actual limiting, averaging, integration or emergence procedure; do not force every procedure to be Wilsonian coarse-graining.
- G: the precise recovered gravitational statement and its assumptions.
- Delta: any justified departure for a defined observable, including parameters and uncertainty or approximation error.

For G use descriptive distinctions, not a score: background/kinematics assumed; continuum action or operator obtained; symmetry-reduced equations; linearized equations on a specified background; or nonlinear equations in a stated domain. Keep matter coupling and the status of the metric variation explicit. A full microscopic-to-GR chain is not presumed.

Map all three units even when an arrow is missing. A pair qualifies for prediction comparison only when both sides support the same observable, a nonempty common physical domain, comparable assumptions/state/matter content and a known parameter/approximation contract. Reference checkpoints cannot supply missing arrows on a competitor's behalf. Failure at a gate is a result about this comparison, not a falsification of the source theory.

### 7.1. Admissibility and symmetric normalization (B1; DM-104)

An admissible parameter set consists only of constraints stated in the pinned source, or strictly entailed by those constraints with the implication recorded, together with the common normalization below. Record the source locator for each constraint; retain unfixed parameters and unbounded ranges. An extractor's preferred cutoff, naturalness assumption, observed bound, convenient example or constraint from another publication is not admissibility evidence. Imported literature constraints require corpus expansion under section 3. Missing dynamics or undefined parameter dependence is UNDERDETERMINED, not an invitation to choose values.

Use a common symbolic low-energy G_star for all units in a comparison where each source defines an effective G and permits that identification. Retain all remaining parameter freedom. This is a symmetric normalization to the same Newton coupling, not an independent determination of G or a numerical measured input. For Lambda, preserve each source's status (input, imposed constraint, derived result or unresolved). A shared Lambda_star, including zero, may be matched only where both source constructions support that common sector; label a constrained zero as imposed, not predicted. Never assign a missing Lambda or overwrite a source constraint to obtain overlap. Record every normalization restriction in the admissible set and label any separation as conditional on that common normalization.

No numerical measurement, fitted spectrum, fitted nonlocality scale, fitted bounce scale, likelihood, or other observational input is permitted. Ordinary units/conventions do not supply additional physical constraints. A source-derived prediction that conflicts with a proposed common normalization cannot simply be matched away: retain that conflict and withhold that normalized comparison. If matching is unsupported, report the relevant failure rather than normalize one side only. For D05/D06/D08, record normalization_status = MATCHED_G, MATCHED_G_AND_LAMBDA, NOT_REQUIRED, NOT_APPLICABLE or UNDETERMINED, the symbolic equalities and source support, and any residual free parameters. The common rule is fixed here; its application must be recorded before selection, not chosen for a favorable separation.

### 7.2. Dispositions and explicit reasons (DM-103)

Use one primary disposition per pair-dimension cell: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE; NO_COMMON_REGIME; UNDERDETERMINED; REPRESENTATION_ONLY; COMPATIBLE_WITHIN_STATED_SCOPE; or CANDIDATE_DISCRIMINATOR. Apply that precedence when more than one defect applies; retain secondary explanations. NOT_SCREENED is the initial state only. A nonzero term in one source and silence in another is UNDERDETERMINED, not a prediction of zero. Locality of a gravitational action and locality of a matter propagator are not the same observable.

Every completed non-candidate cell requires a secondary_reason plus a specific explanation and source/inspection record. Use OUT_OF_CONSTRUCTION_SCOPE when the observable/property is not defined in that construction's stated scope; SOURCE_SILENT when relevant inspected text leaves a potentially in-scope claim unestablished. They are not interchangeable. For other cases use SOURCE_ACCESS_FAILURE, DOMAIN_MISMATCH, SENSE_MISMATCH, PARAMETER_OR_ERROR_UNRESOLVED, STATUS_ONLY, PREDICTION_OVERLAP or CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITY, as appropriate, retaining additional reasons when needed. No generic blank or unexplained OTHER completes a cell. Source inaccessibility is not source silence. Unsupported disjointness is not established compatibility: use UNDERDETERMINED when overlap or separation cannot be established.

### 7.3. Separation scope (B1)

For the same named O and shared conditions C, define each prediction set over its full admissible parameters, including the stated approximation-error allowance. A candidate requires positive source-supported reasoning that the sets are disjoint, not merely different formulas, central values or selected examples. Unknown error control blocks the claimed separation. Retain C and all parameter restrictions explicitly; do not shrink a domain after inspecting agreement merely to force a fork.

Every CANDIDATE_DISCRIMINATOR cell must have separation_scope:

- ROBUST: prediction sets are disjoint over the full admissible sets in the recorded common domain and normalization. Robust means only within this source-specific contract, never across whole theoretical traditions or arbitrary physical regimes.
- CONDITIONAL: the full sets overlap, but disjointness is established in an explicitly located, source-fixed sub-region. Record both the full-set overlap and the exact sub-region, its source locator and why it is fixed by the source rather than by the extractor. Every additional restriction on either side must have that support. A convenient point chosen from a source's allowed range is not a source-fixed sub-region.

A conditional opportunity created by extractor-chosen restrictions is not a candidate: record COMPATIBLE_WITHIN_STATED_SCOPE, secondary_reason = CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITY, and the overlap/restriction. If the underlying prediction or overlap cannot be established, use UNDERDETERMINED instead. Non-candidates record separation_scope = NOT_APPLICABLE with their reason. Initial forms use NOT_SCREENED. A conditional result must never be described as full-admissible-set incompatibility.
