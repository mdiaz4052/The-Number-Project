## Required completion and publication gate

Before claiming a completed screen, check all 30 unit cells, all 30 pair cells and backing records; all six typing records; all nine normalization records; non-candidate reasons; and candidate separation/sub-region support. A justified unresolved/nonapplicable record can complete a non-candidate entry but cannot pass its candidate gate. Missing fields fail completeness. No scientific completeness claim applies to this empty preparation form.

Publish and fetch/read back the complete screen and backing records at an exact GitHub-visible commit before a distinct selection commit. The selection record identifies that commit/digests/readback and chooses the first ROBUST candidate across the entire fixed order; only in their complete absence may the first source-fixed CONDITIONAL candidate be selected. Use the source-location tie rule and preserve unresolved ties rather than make an informal choice. After selection, changed dispositions belong only in the one dossier with reasons, not in rewritten screen evidence; no replacement dossier follows failure.

The eventual dossier also records anticipated_fork_match = YES, NO or UNCERTAIN with the prior-exposure explanation. Source retrieval records distinguish primary versioned e-print archive hashes from secondary PDF hashes and disclose unavailable authentication. These are future recording requirements, not completed source checks.
