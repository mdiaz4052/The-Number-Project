## 2. Prior exposure and interpretation

Candidate families and possible bounce, nonlocality and higher-curvature differences were discussed before registration. Preparatory browsing also exposed abstracts, source versions and limitations. Selection is purposive, unblinded and exploratory; this is not a blind test, systematic survey, novelty claim, or unbiased ranking of theories. Earlier conversational summaries are leads, not evidence. The registration constrains subsequent extraction and comparison; it does not erase prior exposure.

In particular, a cosmological bounce, a nonlocal matter operator and a higher-curvature action term are not automatically mutually exclusive predictions. Recovering selected Friedmann equations, obtaining an averaged continuum curvature operator, and inducing an Einstein-Hilbert term are different achievements. Neither a common action term nor a shared approximate equation proves that the candidates occupy one renormalization-group universality class.

## 5. Common map and eligibility gates

For each unit construct P -> D -> C -> G -> Delta:

- P: primitives, including any geometry or causal structure already assumed.
- D: dynamics, action, operator, measure, state and boundary/initial conditions.
- C: the actual limiting, averaging, integration or emergence procedure; do not force every procedure to be Wilsonian coarse-graining.
- G: the precise recovered gravitational statement and its assumptions.
- Delta: any justified departure for a defined observable, including parameters and uncertainty or approximation error.

For G use descriptive distinctions, not a score: background/kinematics assumed; continuum action or operator obtained; symmetry-reduced equations; linearized equations on a specified background; or nonlinear equations in a stated domain. Keep matter coupling and the status of the metric variation explicit. A full microscopic-to-GR chain is not presumed.

Map all three units even when an arrow is missing. A pair qualifies for prediction comparison only when both sides support the same observable, a nonempty common physical domain, comparable assumptions/state/matter content and a known parameter/approximation contract. Reference checkpoints cannot supply missing arrows on a competitor's behalf. Failure at a gate is a result about this comparison, not a falsification of the source theory.

## 7. Disagreement, parameters and candidate selection

For a potential comparison specify O, the operationally defined observable; C, the shared physical conditions and range; theta_A/theta_B, allowed model parameters; and the predictions with approximation errors. Check units, signs, coordinate/gauge choices, field redefinitions, regulator conventions, boundary terms and state/matter content before interpreting a formal difference physically.

Use one primary disposition per pair-dimension cell: SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE; NO_COMMON_REGIME; UNDERDETERMINED; REPRESENTATION_ONLY; COMPATIBLE_WITHIN_STATED_SCOPE; or CANDIDATE_DISCRIMINATOR. Apply that precedence when more than one defect applies; retain secondary explanations. NOT_SCREENED is the initial state only. A nonzero term in one source and silence in another is UNDERDETERMINED, not a prediction of zero. Locality of a gravitational action and locality of a matter propagator are not the same observable.

A candidate needs two explicit, physically differing predictions in a justified overlap. Distinguish a conditional, parameter-specific difference from separation robust over admissible parameter sets. If allowed parameters make the prediction sets overlap, do not call the construction families incompatible; record the overlap and any conditional discriminatory opportunity. Do not extrapolate a low-energy expansion to its cutoff or a condensate approximation outside its stated control simply to create a fork.

After the complete screen, choose at most one eligible candidate by the first dimension and then first pair in the fixed order above. Report the other eligible cells but do not develop additional candidate studies. This is an ordering rule for a bounded pilot, not a discovery of the physically earliest scale. If the chosen candidate fails detailed scrutiny, retain its failure and stop rather than shopping for a replacement within revision 1.
